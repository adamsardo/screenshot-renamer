# Privacy and file safety

Screenshot Renamer processes images using Apple frameworks on your Mac. It has no network client, network entitlement, analytics, account system or third-party AI endpoint. Apple controls the availability and downloads of its system model.

The app receives access only to files/folders you choose or drop. Persisted security-scoped folder bookmarks let you undo after a restart. To revoke access, remove the app's sandbox container after saving anything you want to keep; this also removes history. macOS may ask for access again after an ad-hoc preview is replaced with a different build.

History is local to the Mac and stores original/destination filenames, dates, file identifiers and content hashes. It does not store image copies, model conversations or recognised text. Filenames themselves can reveal information. Clear History removes the recovery records and prevents undo for those batches. It does not change the files.

AI output is a suggestion. The prompt asks the model to avoid personal identifiers and secrets, but this is not a guarantee. Review names before applying, particularly for screenshots containing messages, account information or sensitive documents.

No app can guarantee recovery if the source has been removed, edited or moved outside its access. The app reports conflicts and leaves existing files intact. Keep normal backups for valuable data.
